# KORE Positioning

## One-liner
KORE is the screenless focus system that helps students reset cognitive strain and sustain high-quality study blocks without relying on nicotine or stimulants.

## Problem
Students are overloaded and self-medicate with caffeine and nicotine to stay on task. Existing tools either demand high effort (meditation) or deliver passive, generic content with no proof of impact.

## Solution
KORE pairs a wearable patch with a minimal app to detect focus strain and deliver a low-friction reset protocol. It focuses on clarity, steadier attention, and a measurable return to focus.

## Core Loop
1. Detect strain or focus drift.
2. Trigger a short reset protocol.
3. Confirm uplift with a simple check-in.
4. Reinforce with streaks and recovery trends.

## Differentiators
- Closed-loop feedback instead of passive media.
- Personal baseline and adaptive thresholds.
- Low friction for stressed users.
- Clear, measurable focus outcomes.

## What We Are Not
- Not a medical device in this phase.
- Not a replacement for clinical care.
- Not a generic meditation app.

## Key Metrics
- Focus session completion rate.
- Reset effectiveness (pre vs post clarity delta).
- Weekly focus minutes and streaks.
- 7- and 14-day retention.

## Messaging Principles
- Wellness and performance language only.
- No medical or treatment claims.
- Emphasize clarity, calm, and sustained focus.
