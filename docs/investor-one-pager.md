# KORE Investor One-Pager

## Company
KORE builds a screenless focus system that helps students reset cognitive strain and sustain high-quality study blocks without relying on nicotine or stimulants.

## Problem
Academic pressure is rising while attention is fragmenting. Students often rely on stimulants or nicotine rituals to push through heavy workloads, which worsens anxiety and dependence.

## Solution
KORE combines a wearable patch with a minimal app to detect focus strain and guide short reset protocols. The experience is low-friction, personalized, and measurable.

## KORE 1: The Real Enemy of Focus
When students lose focus, the issue is not an empty mind but a noisy one. Competing neural activity lowers signal-to-noise ratio, so the task signal cannot win.

## How the Patch Activates Focus (tACS Loop)
- Patch emits a low-beta / high-alpha sinusoidal microcurrent (~10 to 15 Hz).
- Resting membrane potentials in the dlPFC are gently nudged to align with the wave.
- Neurons entrain to a shared rhythm within minutes.
- The prefrontal cortex applies top-down control and closes the sensory gate to distractions.

## Result
Frictionless focus without stimulant-like jitter. The environment feels quieter, and cravings or notification impulses are structurally dampened.

## Neurochemistry (Why It Can Replace Vapes)
Non-invasive stimulation research indicates that prefrontal modulation can shift monoamine signaling. KORE is designed to nudge those pathways in the direction of calm and stable drive.
- Serotonin regulation supports anxiety reduction, mood stability, and a true "brain reset" window.
- Dopamine balance supports focus and motivation without a sharp external spike.
- The loop targets endogenous regulation rather than chemical dumping, aiming to reduce dependency cycles.

## Simple Message
Your brain loses focus when it gets too noisy. KORE acts like noise-canceling headphones for your thoughts, syncing your executive control center into a unified rhythm so distractions and cravings fade out.

## Product
- Wearable patch for passive sensing and ritual.
- App with Cognitive Load Index and one-tap reset.
- Post-session check-ins to validate uplift.

## Why Now
- Wearables are normalized for performance tracking.
- Students actively seek alternatives to nicotine and stimulants.
- AI-native personalization enables real-time adaptation.

## Differentiation
- Closed-loop feedback vs passive content.
- Personalized baseline vs one-size-fits-all guidance.
- Low-friction reset vs high-effort meditation.
- Neurosignal to neurochemistry framing for nicotine-replacement behavior.

## Initial Traction Goal
- 20 to 30 students in a single campus cohort.
- 2 to 3 weeks of daily usage.
- 30 percent say they would be very disappointed if removed.

## Business Model
Hardware plus subscription for analytics and protocols.

## Roadmap
- v0.1: App-only prototype with guided reset protocol.
- v0.2: Wearable form factor and passive sensing.
- v1: Closed-loop personalization and refined UX.

## Ask
Pilot partners and pre-seed funding to prove retention and refine the reset protocol.
