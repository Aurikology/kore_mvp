# KORE 🧠🚀

> **The Digital Dashboard for Your Mind.** A high-performance, non-invasive neurotechnology platform designed to monitor cognitive load, eliminate burnout, and provide a healthy, bio-engineered replacement for chemical dependencies like vapes and nicotine pouches.

---

## 📋 Project Overview

KORE is an "always-on" wearable brain patch paired with an AI-native cross-platform application. While college students increasingly turn to chemical stimulants or habits to power through academic stress, KORE aims to rewrite that paradigm. By utilizing **Closed-Loop Transcranial Alternating Current Stimulation (tACS)** and real-time **Electroencephalography (EEG)** tracking, KORE gives students a literal "brain reset"—helping them master their focus, safely lower anxiety, and break away from addictive substances.

### Key Visual Language & Aesthetic
Inspired by ultra-clean, minimal corporate design (think *WHOOP* or *Apple*), the user interface emphasizes readability, removing visual clutter for users who are already experiencing extreme cognitive fatigue.

---

## 🔬 The Science (How it Works)

KORE operates as a dynamic, **closed-loop system** acting directly through the scalp to interface with the biological hardware of the prefrontal cortex:

1. **Sensing (EEG Micro-volt Analysis):** The hardware nodes actively track raw microvolt potentials from the **Dorsolateral Prefrontal Cortex (dlPFC)**. It continuously monitors alpha suppression (focus loss) and spikes in frontal theta waves (working memory capacity overload).
2. **AI-Native Prediction:** Local and cloud-based models map incoming telemetry into an abstract **"Cognitive Bandwidth Meter"** (0–100 score) before a complete focus crash occurs.
3. **Targeted Neuromodulation (The Reset):** When a user hits peak cognitive strain or signals a substance craving, the patch initiates a painless, ultra-low microcurrent session (< 2mA). This initiates **neural entrainment** at a baseline alpha frequency (10 Hz), shifting the brain from a frantic fight-or-flight beta-overload into deep, intentional restoration.
